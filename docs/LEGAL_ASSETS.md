# IP / asset licensing gate

The intended inspiration is Tibia, but **inspiration ≠ permission to distribute artwork, sounds, client sprites, logos, names, maps or other protected assets**. Before including any original Tibia resources, obtain documented written permission from the rights holder covering browser-game reuse, derivative changes, worldwide display, redistribution, and commercial use if relevant. Do not assume fansites/OTS use proves legal entitlement.

## Strict workflow
1. Inventory proposed assets in `assets/ASSET_REGISTER.csv` with owner, source, license terms, evidence URL/file, allowed use, approved_by, and status.
2. Mark any unknown/third-party resources `BLOCKED` until authorized; do not commit assets nor fetch them in runtime without permissions.
3. Use original commissioned/licensed pixel art as fallback. Do not ask a generative model to reproduce recognizable protected Tibia sprites 1:1.
4. Avoid implying official association with CipSoft; use independent branding and a clear disclaimer, reviewed legally.
5. Product mechanics as generic ideas are different from copying original expression: assess names, story, trade dress and visual similarity before release.

**Product decision needed:** if owner chooses Tibia originals, this becomes a legal milestone/blocker, not a silent engineering assumption.
